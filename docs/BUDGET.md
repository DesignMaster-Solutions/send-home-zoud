## Assumptions

The AI agent speeds up the code, so most funded hours go to what it cannot do alone: playtests with people, native
language review, work with trainers, art, music and store releases. There are no other cost lines because our store
accounts already exist, the web build stays on free hosting and the corridor data is public.

## Milestones

- Month 1: first playtest on the web build, corridor data file, native review of the Arabic.
- Month 2: design and balance from the playtest, Hindi, Urdu and Filipino texts.
- Month 3: session mode with trainers, second playtest.
- Month 4: art and sound, mobile builds.
- Month 5: QA on low-end phones, offline play, third playtest.
- Month 6: store release and the playtest report (what players got right before and after playing).

## What ships at each funding level

Prizes and final amounts are set by the jam, so the plan is cut from the bottom of its priority list, not spread thin.
Learning and reach come first; new content comes last.

| $30,000 | Lines 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 = $23,850 + 10% contingency = $26,235 | Plus the session mode and the iOS and Android release with the current art |

## What stays free

The web version stays free to play, with no ads and no data collection from players.
