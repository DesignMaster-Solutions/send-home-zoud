## Short description

Payday in Dubai, family back home. Read past the ZERO FEE signs and count what really reaches them.

## Description

<p><strong>Send Home</strong> is a financial literacy game made for Zoud GameLab (theme: Make FinLit Fun).</p>
<p>You work in Dubai and your family lives in India, Pakistan or the Philippines. Every payday you choose how much to send and how much to keep as a cushion for trouble here. Then you go to the street of exchange counters. Every counter shows a fee and a rate. The loud kiosk shouts ZERO FEE, and its rate quietly takes more. The only number that matters is what your family receives, and the counters close for Friday prayer at 12:45.</p>
<p>A friend of a friend on the phone offers cash at a better rate, no receipt. It works, until the day it does not. Messages from home ask for school fees, a roof before the rains, a family festival (Diwali, Eid or Christmas), a hospital bill. A salary arrives a month late once a year. Four endings, more than one of them good.</p>
<p><strong>What you learn by playing:</strong> the total cost of a transfer is the fee plus the exchange-rate margin, measured against the mid-market rate; a receipt after every transfer shows both. Sending often pays the fee every time. A cushion keeps one bad month from becoming debt. A licensed channel gives you a receipt you can claim.</p>
<p><strong>Real numbers, cited in the repository:</strong> the World Bank's Remittance Prices Worldwide put the average cost of sending AED 735 from the UAE in early 2025 at 3.68% to India, 1.42% to Pakistan and 3.04% to the Philippines; the UN goal (SDG 10.c) is under 3% by 2030. The counters and apps in the game are fictional.</p>
<p><strong>UAE edition and accessibility:</strong> English and Arabic (right to left), money in dirhams. Settings on the title screen: large text, high contrast, calm (no clock), plain words, sound off; P or Esc pauses on the street.</p>
<p>About 8 minutes for a year. Plays on phones and desktops. Made by Design Master Solutions. Code: <a href="https://github.com/DesignMaster-Solutions/send-home-zoud">github.com/DesignMaster-Solutions/send-home-zoud</a>.</p>

## Instructions (on the page, as the last paragraph of the description)

<p>Tap or click. On each payday, move the slider (or tap Enough or All) to choose what to send, then go to the street. Tap a counter to ask for a quote (it takes 2 minutes of the queue), then send there or look elsewhere. Read the receipt. P or Esc pauses on the street.</p>

## Jam form: Making FinLit Fun

Every payday you choose what to send home and what to keep, then you walk a street of exchange counters before they close for Friday prayer at 12:45. Each counter shows a fee and a rate; the loud kiosk shouts ZERO FEE and its rate takes the most. You ask for quotes against the clock, send, and a receipt splits the cost into the fee and the margin hidden in the rate, measured against the mid-market rate and the UN goal of 3%. A friend of a friend on the phone offers a better rate in cash, no receipt, until the day the money never arrives. Trouble here comes out of your cushion or becomes a salary advance with a charge. Players learn by playing: compare what the family receives, not the fee; keep a cushion; use a licensed channel that gives a receipt.
